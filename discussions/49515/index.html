↳ Posted by 🎻Johnny Jay, Melody Player 🎻 on August 19th, 2024.

Re: Tunes you don’t like playing.

I learned a long time ago not to publicly disclose the tunes I don’t like, because it leaves an opening for my friends to slag me by starting tunes they know I don’t like! ;-)

But I will say this in general, the tunes that I tend to dislike playing are usually tunes that I think have basically no nutritional value. If they’re overly repetitive and don’t do anything that I consider to be remotely interesting, I cringe when they come up in sessions, but I’ll still play them (mostly so other people don’t know that I don’t like them…)

I do generally dislike playing tunes that have been beat to death, but in most of those cases, they’re actually great tunes, and when they come up in sessions after it has been a long time since I played them, it’s easy for me to spot why they got so popular. The nice thing is that both of the sessions that I anchor tend to consciously work on playing a variety of tunes, and not let anything get too stale. So when I’m in a session at a festival, I might end up playing tons of the “common” tunes that I haven’t played in months or years, and it doesn’t bother me because it’s like visiting with an old friend I haven’t seen forever.

Another thing I realized years ago is that when someone brings up an old standard tune, I don’t have to play it the same way as I used to when I first learned the tune (likely in the first few years of playing). I can amuse myself by finding new ways to express the tune in a more interesting way (similar to the video above of Shannon Heaton playing the Irish Washerwoman), while making sure I’m not stepping on anybody else’s (often mindless) regurgitation of the same old same old…
