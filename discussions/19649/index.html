A Chance to Meet at Ennis Trad Fest

Has everyone seen page http://www.ennistradfestival.com/top_level/sessions.asp which outlines sessions over the five days and nights of the Ennis Trad Fest next weekend – with all that choice, this has to be as close as one gets to a sessioneer’s paradise!

My only crib is that with so many sessions going on, it will be well nigh impossible to have a tune with any fellow Yellowboarders. For anyone who’d like a few tunes in an open and very democratic session I’d therefore suggest the Friday night in Cois na hAbhna. This session will be similar Larry Reynolds’ Green Briar session in Boston. The first two hours (8.00 to 10.00pm) will be a Slow Session and the tune sets will be pre-determined while from 10.00pm onwards, normal speed will be resumed and it’s every man (or woman) for himself!

For my sins I’ve been drafted in to assist with this event and have put together a few suggested tune sets which can be found on the website at http://comhaltas.ie/shop/detail/foinn_seisiun_book_volume_1/ if anyone wants to have a listen in advance. The sets include a variety of dance tunes as follows:-

Jigs
Haunted House
My Darling Asleep
Club Céilí

Polkas
Little Diamond Polka
Murroe Polka
Maid of Ardath

Reels
Boyne Hunt
Shannon Breeze
Red-Haired Lass

Slides
Brosna Slide
O’Keefe’s
Dennis Murphy’s

Mazurkas
John Doherty’s Mazurka
Vincent Campbell’s

Hornpipes
Sonny Murray’s
Home Ruler
Kitty’s Wedding

The session is definitely intended to be “open” and all musicians are welcome; as for the “democratic” aspect, additional/alternative tune sets may be suggested here for the Slow Session bit, but only from people who intend on being there.

Finally I hope that people attending continue to respect each others anonymity on the site – we don’t want posts next week saying “I heard xxxx playing at the Trad Fest session and he was totally c**p”!

ALL the sessions are gigs at Ennis and hardly any of them are open as Bannerman suggests. If you want a truly inclusive festival scene head for the Cooley/Collins festival October bank holiday or Derrygonnelly in Fermanagh, or Drumshanbo for Joe Mooney/Packie Duignan and the difference is clear to anyone with eyes or ears. In these places there is a genuine welcome for musicians of all standards and the ensuing craic is mighty.
