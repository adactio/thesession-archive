Re: February blahs

Rather than the “February blahs”, for me it’s more like the calm before the storm, i.e. a chance to rest up and chill out before the madness of the St. Patrick’s Day season. And I’m not one who plays gigs, either, so for me it’s just the fact that all the Irish bands tour during the few weeks before and after March 17th. Schedules seem to be firming up later than usual this year, as just last week it looked like nothing was happening, save for a Lunasa gig that was already sold out before I even found out about it. (Bummer…) But just in the past few days I’ve found out about (and gone broke buying tickets for) the following shows happening in just a couple of weeks this March: Teada, Liz Carroll and John Doyle, Karan Casey, Dervish and Cathie Ryan. And as for cleaning house, I’ve been reminded of that too, since four years ago when I bought my house I established a tradition of having a tune party near the end of March, and another one in the fall around Halloween time. As I think I mentioned in another thread, this guarantees that my house will be clean at least twice each year. Well, the other day one of my session-mates said, “Hey, it’s about time for your party again, isn’t it?” Oops - that means it’s time to clean house again! But I can’t do it quite yet, ’cause it would just be dirty again by the end of March. So yet another thing to be taken care of in the midst of the March storm, which makes me enjoy the February calm right now. (And thank God that I seem to have missed the killer flu this winter, knock on wood…)

But in Arizona I imagine YMMV, Emily. Do any bands even tour there? But here’s a CD recommendation that always gets me moving: Micheal O Raighaillagh’s “The Nervous Man”. It’s the one that first got me interested in concertina, although there’s no way I’ll ever be able to play like him…

Hey Em, well done for taking yourself off for a trip to town. I’ve never been deprived of rain myself, but all the same, I seem to like it more and more.
