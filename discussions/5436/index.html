Re: Thesession members meeting up

Well Tom, I reckon you would be fine at any session, if you have been practising hard for months. This is the whole point of it. Being a late starter as a musician, I spent a year going to a session as a listener, then going home to practise the tunes and only joined the session upon being asked. A lot of us do that.
The problem is with people who cannot see how a particular session works, i.e. don’t listen to what’s being played and how, and don’t feel that they should practise and learn the session tunes, so that they could be part of it.
Joining a session, as a possible regular or a visiting musician is a bit playing a team game. There are others to consider, that’s all.
