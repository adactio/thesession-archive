Re: Using AI to illustrate tune names

Although people may be impressed by the complexity and legibility of Chat-GPT’s output, I do not think it will ever be a threat to Irish traditional music. The gatekeepers of the music, the musicians themselves, will always have the final say on what tunes are played, and they are unlikely to choose an AI generated tune. In Irish traditional music, tunes are often chosen based on their history and association with well-respected players, as well as their unique style. Even if an AI generated tune was well composed, it would not have the same history and commentary associated with it, making it less desirable to play. Therefore, even if Chat-GPT were to produce high-quality tunes, they would still be considered trite in the context of Irish traditional music.

Re: Using AI to illustrate tune names

Just a reminder for those who don’t realize what this AI software is doing:

It’s not a general intelligence AI that thinks like we do. It’s software that has been “trained” on everything that can be scraped off the Internet that humans have written online. Vast amounts of data, making logical associations within those patterns in response to text input. What you’re seeing here is basically chopped up and reassembled versions of conversations that humans have made in the past and posted online, with value weighting based on the input. That doesn’t make it any less scary and mind-blowing, of course.

English teachers will have to stop asking kids to write essay questions for a test, and that’s just the start of where this is going. It’s going to hit the music world sooner or later with music composing. I’m hoping our particular weird ITM niche will be more resistant than the Pop music world.
