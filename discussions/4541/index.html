Tonite I am having a nice Napa Valley Cabernet and making a Thai dish, Larb.

Not a very appetizing name is it? Good though.

This is one of our all-time favorite Thai dishes, and it is a very common dish served throughout Thailand as well as Laos. Larb is an easy, quick to make “spicy” dish (it can be, and often is fierily hot). It can be found on Thai restaurant menus in America as “chicken salad Thai style”, which might be the best description for this dish.

Re: What’s for dinner tonite?

I make brown rice a special way … first I sautee it in butter to bring out the sugars. Then I flash boil it (I add the correct amount of boiling water to the sauteed rice) and let it boil like crazy for about 2 minutes -- this builds back the flavor that was released into the kernels. I cover the pot for 45 minutes at low heat -- absolutely no checking allowed, then open and stir it immediately.

Then I add a very sharp cheddar, some tofu, some sprouts, a bit of cayenne pepper, some brewer’s yeast, and some soy sauce as toppings.

This one yummy dish was tonight’s dinner.

The other half made his famous tortilla skillet tonight. Brown ground meat (in this case pork sausage, although usually he uses ground buffalo or ground turkey) and onions in a cast iron skillet until the onions get soft. Cut up 6 or so small corn tortillas and throw them in. When the lot is almost done, throw in refried beans to taste and some choppedtomato. As soon as the beans are hot, serve with a vegetable (fresh green beans from yesterday’s farmer’s market) and fruit for dessert (the last of this year’s peaches, from said market). Yum! “Guy food” at its best.

When I’m not too reactive allergy-wise we also throw on some grated goat milk cheddar for a garnish, and sometimes some chopped avocado. But I’m reactive tonight *sniffle* and we’re out of avocados, which are too rich to serve with pork anyway.
