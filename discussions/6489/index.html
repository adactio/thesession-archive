How good are you?

In the spirit of Kerri’s “tell it as it is” how good a musician are you?

No false modesty just an honest, perhaps self conscious, opinion, self analysis.

Take me as an example.

Blues Harp: reasonably good, but I don’t play it enough, and don’t play enough blues.

Whistle: Only play one tune, “Lonesome Boatman” which can be good, albeit shrill.

Mandolin: OK for a “folk group” songs, slow airs and Carolan stuff, but could not really be called good.

Guitar: God almighty awful, but I enjoy playing it and singing along in the house, and for photographs on websites.

Bodhran: Kelly, Mercier,, McDonagh, Hayes, Quinn, I’m in that league, albeit on a humble instrument.

Now, what about the rest of you?
