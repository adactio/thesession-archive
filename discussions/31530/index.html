Re: Musical Squeezebox Dream: When you see my music video, you will understand. …

Hey Dave, Thanks again for your encouragement and support. I decided to pop over here to check out the site you mentioned, The Session. Wow. I guess you are one of the very few who managed to get a chuckle out of my admittedly absurd concertina campaign. But what’s that old saying, you can’t please all the people all of the time. Lol.

Anyway, I’d like to clear a few things up for the record. First, I am aware of the student models mentioned by Heike. And I’m actually planning a trip down to The Button Box in Massachusetts in two weeks to rent one. So I am very excited about that. Second, I do play clubs in a band, however I also have a day job working as a courier. Most of my survival cash comes from that.

So what possessed me to explore the extremes of impertinence with my highly offensive concertina campaign? Well, let me tell you. I happened to see the video of Drunken Lullabies by Flogging Molly on YouTube and it blew me away. I loved the energy and the vibe and the Celtic goodness. It made me really want to write some sort of rowdy Irish bar song. And since I had been researching concertinas and bemoaning the expense of them, it seemed like a perfect subject for my first Irish ditty. And I thought that maybe if I worked hard at writing and recording a funny song and making a video for it, maybe there would be some people who would get a chuckle and throw a buck or two in my instrument case to help me save up for an actual concertina. I figured it was an extreme long shot. But no matter what happened, it would be fun to do. And it was. I like the song. I play it on my ukulele all the time.

What I never expected was how offended some people would get over it. I’m fine with the campaign fizzling out as a crazy idea. But, I’m kind of surprised and saddened by the negativity it has brought out in some people. Nobody is twisting anybody’s arm here. It was just a wacky, absurd attempt to see if my silly song and video could somehow result in an actual instrument. Nobody gets hurt. You just click your mouse button and the video goes away.

Anyway, I am very excited about renting a concertina in two weeks. I’m thinking I will go with an Elise Duet.

Thanks again, Dave, for sticking your neck out on my behalf. Hopefully we’ll be able to have the cup of tea soon! :)

Go Cat Dave, First, I heartily applaud and highly recommend you carry out your plan to go to the Button Box. They are great people, and there are not too many places left who have a wide selection of instruments you can try, CDs and music books to learn from, and will not only sell you an instrument, but will be available to service and retune it in the future. I do have an ulterior motive in recommending them, as I want them to stay in business for many years to come!
Second, I hope you understand how cheeky it looks to people who have immersed themselves in a particular genre of music for years to hear a ditty that was written by someone who has barely been exposed to that genre. To them, the word “Celtic” is an over-generalization generally used by marketing people, not those who truly love and appreciate the music. I appreciate your enthusiasm, but you also need to realize that uneducated enthusiasm can be grating at times.
But, that being said, you are poised to learn some great music, and how to play it on a great little instrument. You have many of years of fun ahead of you.
Enjoy!
