Can of worms:- Tunes section:- Is my frustration in vain?

I am so hesitant to re-open this can of worms, as I guess it goes awfully close to a meta-discussion, but I must take my chance:- (and I will readily accept the referee’s decision).
Is it not the case that the tunes section is supposed to represent tunes that are *already* accepted as session tunes? We all know the rules regarding posting *ones own* originals and paying for it with 5 or so versions of other tunes, but it seems to me that lately, and with growing speed, the tunes section is getting bogged down with modern and often mediocre compositions not known to sessioners. Should they not, as with all other tunes, first have to prove themselves session worthy? In my opinion, some of them are outright NOT session worthy, and don’t even belong here. Now, I know there is no black and white solution. I understand that Jeremy has been very tolerant of accepting a wide range of, as yet, non-session tune submissions, but is there any line that can be drawn? I must admit that I can’t imagine one, but my frustration makes me ask.

Re: Can of worms:- Tunes section:- Is my frustration in vain?

This site is for traditional Irish tunes (stating the obvious), and I think the content reflects this. I compose tunes too, and although they roughly follow the format of Irish tunes (A part, B part, common number of bars, regular time sig, etc), many of them sound nothing like Irish tunes at all, and they are not meant to.

I’ve submitted a few of them here, but I’ve submitted more trad tune versions, so I’ve kind of adhered to the rule/recommendation.

There’s a bit of a catch-22 situation here too. Non-trad tunes do get played at sessions, but only if they are known, but they won’t be known unless you submit them here, or people learn them from high profile players.

So, a non-trad tune by someone like Michael McGoldrick will easily make it to the tune database, and no one will complain, whereas an original comp by a member might raise the question of “where’s your five trad ones?”, regardless of the quality of the tune.

Not a complaint, btw, just an observation.

Re: Can of worms:- Tunes section:- Is my frustration in vain?

You don’t need to worry about ‘originals’, Gobby; look upon them as vanity publishing. Someone has slung together a few notes they believe constitute a tune that the world should hear; but they haven’t the conviction/ability to play it at a session so they dump it in the mustard in the hope its light will shine. The fact is that it will drift/plummet to the bottom and disappear into the all enveloping silt. Of course, it’s possible, it may get noticed; in the literary world there are numerous self publishers who have risen: Beatrix Potter, Marel Proust, Edgar Allen Poe, etc. But they are exceptions and I doubt that many of the original tunes here come into that category. Stop looking at new submissions; it’ll only give you palpitations.
