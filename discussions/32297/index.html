↳ Posted by Davidread on August 14th, 2013.

Re: Uilleann Pipes from Hungary

Dear All,

My attention was called on this topic, and I was reading it with an abashed face…
From day to day, unfortunately, it is more clear to me that now, the most challenging for a pipe maker is keeping up with all that (many times discrediting and unfair) information which is about himself. There is a growing number of rumor due to impatience.

The instrument mentioned was sent much before the topic was created. According to the date shown by the tracking system it arrived on the same day with the creation of the topic. That time, I was in a distant part of my county at a folklore festival with a group of Croatian pipers. It was located in a place with no internet access. Actually, I go to such places frequently, as I play in several formations, and communication is not always easy.

In connection with that case with “Ted”, I think there in no need for further comments from my part. If someone is interested, can use the forums and fair enough for creating opinion with a proper backing, he or she will find material to read.

And it is very sad and unfortunate, that there are still misunderstandings and rumors caused by the cancelation of my previous mailbox. It was 3 (or more) years ago, and I could do nothing for saving it, it just happened - thanks to the server. I lost all my contacts and some went upset, thinking I was hiding from them…

So please always get informed before sharing someone’s opinion, and believe, one who puts everything on his name will never risk it intentionally.

Thanks for reading.

All the best,
Richard
