Re: Best app to remind me of the first few notes of a tune

An issue which many of us have.

Presumably, it’s when you are asked to start a tune yourself(or want to) that you sometimes forget the first couple of notes?

The old fashioned way was to keep a small note pad and you could compile a list of more than enough “intros” that way. Less distracting for the other players too.

Usually, I don’t worry about such things although I might scribble down the names of a couple of tunes/sets I particularly want to play in case my mind goes blank. Usually, if I remember the titles, everything else falls into place.

Re: Best app to remind me of the first few notes of a tune

I’ve a cheap and cheerful small pocket notebook. Generally one SET per page. Batteries/internet not required.

Names of the two/three tunes in the set. “Key” beside name in brackets [key]. Followed by first two bars in simplified ABC (plus any lead-in notes but now I try to separate them because sometimes dropped on switch between sets). All written out nice and big so that I can read easily or reference with a glance on Tune 1.. Tune 2.. Tune 3.

The exception to the “set per page” is for things like Carolan Tunes, Waltzes or “off piste” tunes - these have a double page spread with just the individual incipit ABCs to get me started.

I have the same problem. I tried printing off a blank tab sheet and filling in the first few notes but that doesn’t give you any idea of how the tune sounds.
My solution was to use the voice recorder on my phone. Record the first few notes or the A part…or the whole tune if you like. Then add the title and I have an alphabetical list in voice recorder on my phone. It’s quite easy to listen with the volume turned right down as you only need the first half dozen notes and you’ve got it. Works for me.

How about the “B”?

Maybe it’s just a personal quirk that won’t work for all but I noticed long ago that if I couldn’t conjure the first notes of a tune I could often get a phrase or two and/or bits of the second half. Even more helpful are the final notes/phrase of that part. Mentally playing this gets me back to the start point of any repeating tune which I can then remember.

I’d say if I can remember any small coherent bit of any tune this works…at least for me….about 90% of the time. I have no idea why. That said—and for the other 10% of the time—I also carry around a little notebook with the first few bars of many tunes jotted down. Paper + pencils…what’ll they dream up next?
