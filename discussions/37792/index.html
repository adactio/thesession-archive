So I guess no option for going from one tune to the other unless it from an accustomed set?

I used to go to a session years ago, and there was a fiddle player who came, he played mostly old time stuff but was a fantastic player, and as time passed he made a great effort to pick up more and more Irish tunes, as well as bringing different tunes to the session that fit in quite nicely. And, he was a great guy to boot! (a good indicator, he came with a friend who was a session regular on his first night, sat and listened for a good while, then respectfully asked what the protocol was! And his playing was top notch! It was impossible to follow anything he played up with another tune. But a small complaint indeed for an otherwise great addition to the session, and nothing compared to the frustration of what you’re mentioning. Are all of these people players of Irish music? Or do they primarily play other stuff and come out because they have heard tunes before and it would be fun to play? Maybe they are unfamiliar with the protocol?

I am not a huge stickler for having to do things “the right way” all the time, don’t death-stare someone of they sneak out some sheet music from time to time, and the occasional tune from different styles are fine with me, I enjoy them! But there has to be some adherence to some sort of custom, and in my mind the night is best if there is variation and surprises and great energy, and it sounds like this whole music stand arranged business would crush the soul right out of it. And I love Irish tunes! If I wanted to hear bluegrass or free-flowing new age improvisation all night I would seek it out, but I don’t, and I go out to play Irish tunes. For some reason, people think music is music, and why not play whatever or however you want? For those folks I liken it to sports - if a bunch of people get together to play football and some guy show up swinging a hockey stick around or strings a net up in the middle of the field, it kinda wrecks it, no? If you want to play hockey or volleyball or Calvinball feel free to get a bunch of people together and do so, but why come in and try to take over our football game?

So I assume this session was ok before, and has regressed to what it is now? Or was it like that when you started going to it?
