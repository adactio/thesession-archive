Complete the Sentence….

The other night I was sitting next to a beginning u-piper who sounded more like one who slaughters geese. I was being kind and did my best to make music on my whistle. I was very indulgent thru the squawks and squeaks and, sadly, an out of tune set of pipes. No biggy. We all have to start somewhere. Out of the blue, he turns to me and says:

“You should take up something more challenging”.

And I said:

“………..what should I have said……………”
