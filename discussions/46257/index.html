“Freedom” to play in sessions or just an increased risk for many?

Sorry for yet another Covid related thread…..

The UK Govt are keen to remove all remaining restrictions on July 19th. This even includes the mandatory wearing of face masks in enclosed public spaces.

Presumably, this means that all sessions could resume as so called “normal activity” returns. However, I feel that this so called “freedom” actually excludes large numbers of the population who might be quite vulnerable health wise or still concerned about venturing out and about or visiting busy indoor venues especially without some protection.

“Here in Scotland”, it is expected that a more cautious approach will be taken and I would agree that this is much more sensible. Surely, it’s better to have a more gradual and, ideally, an inclusive approach than just to throw caution to the wind? Full freedom(Not that there’s such a thing) for one section of the population will inevitably adversely affect and restrict the activities of many others.

Of course, I want things to return to normal as soon as possible and I will still (As a double vaccinated person) continue to socialise. However, I will probably choose which sessions and other live events to attend with care. It’s not so much my fellow musicians who worry me but everyone else who may be wandering around in the pub and so on.
I also think it’s probably OK to be seated in a session or concert without a mask but I feel that they should still be worn when moving around. Also, some distance should still be maintained and “huddling together” in corner still seems a little risky to me.

Anyway, how do you all feel about this? Are you “raring to go” or, possibly, feeling even less safe than before?
