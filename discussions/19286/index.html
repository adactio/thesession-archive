Re: irish traditional music playing to fast in thondon sessions

I can keep up with Brian (not always gracefully…LOL) and I am a pretty mediocre player. I’ve also had some great craic at Waxy’s.

Like Conan said, they are not playing for dancers so it doesn’t matter if they are playing faster than competition dance speed. Who cares? They are enjoying themselves!

I understand that warp speed sessions are not to everyone’s taste and I have heard the argument put forth by musos of all levels that Irish music *should* be played at a laid-back tempo. I like laidback, swingy sessions as well as intense, high speed ones, and I cringe a bit when I hear it *should* be one way or another. The old boys like Paddy Killoran and Patsy Touhy knew how to put it into fifth gear so there is plenty of historical justification (if you really want it) for playing fast. It’s a stylistic choice you make and it is fair enough to say that blazing speed doesn’t appeal to you personally. We all have our preferences.

In another thread someone mentioned a difference between people who dislike speed because it is just a style they’re not keen on and people who dislike speed because they cannot play that fast. I just thought I’d flag that point.

Also, I hate to be the grammar police but it is more difficult to read and understand a post with no punctuation than it is to play a session in the Cobden (and a lot less fun).
