If you were a tune, what would your name be…

Inspired by Silver Spears recent post:
"I wouldn’t mind being a tune: existing in a state of non-existence
until some musicians suddenly, with a few twiddles of their fingers, bring you into being."

So if you were a tune, what tune would you be?
What tune best describes your personality, the you of you?

Say you had an explosive temper, would you be The Tempest?
If you loved to sing, would you be The Opera Reel, or The Chorus Jig?
Say you loved your drink, but your doctor (and your liver) forced you to give it up.
Would you be Farewell To Whiskey, or the Temperance Reel?
