Session Relegation

I was prompted to consider this after reading the “Wayne Rooney” post and wading through lengthy past posts regarding session “etiquette” and various anti-(insert your favorite rant here) threads. Why not implement a system of relegation like they do in professional football/futbol/soccer leagues around the world?

In each town of size there is usually a “beginner” session, and intermediate session, and a “premiere” session. My theory is, at the end of the summer, The best two players from the lower tiers are promoted, whereas the slugs who never learn any knew tunes, continue to noodle, drink from another’s pint, play the same sets over and over again, etc, etc, are relegated to the lower sessions. I think this would tidy things up immensely.

(DISCLAIMER - For those literalists who take everything on the session.org seriously, or for those who have no knowledge whatsoever of Football/Futbol/Soccer, I am only joking)
