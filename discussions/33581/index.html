Škoda ~ writing off a whole make because of the poor sound!!!

http://www.skoda.co.uk/

We were particularly interested in the Yeti. But, I had my doubts from the start, with regards to the in-built sound system. Some daft nut had tucked the main speaker into the lower back of the front doors, facing in to the driver’s and passenger’s seat, low down. Along the upper door and forward, below the window, were tweeters, something that all the cars we’ve test driven had. One might be impressed with the cute kit, the touch screen, which we found a bit fiddly, and that you could load 6 CDs, which it held firmly on to when ejecting them. So, we took out their top of the range and loaded six CDs.

I was holding back on analysis but my wife immediately commented that she didn’t like it, and that the tinniness of it even gave her a headache. The whole trip she complained about the sound, and we had quite a wide variety of things to listen to, nine CDs in total, including “Ego Trip”, just mentioned above, and the Santana recording, and not forgetting a bit of Bach too. I knew, from her displeasure, that Škoda was no longer a consideration. There was also a bit of the old diesel noises, something I was familiar with, but which most of the new diesels don’t have. I was also surprised that we no longer needed to wait for a light to go off before turning over the engine.

Old diesel noise, road noise, I enjoyed the drive, but I have to agree with my main co-pilot, even trying to adjust treble, mid and bass in different configurations, mainly trying to cut the treble and boost the bass ~ the sound sucked! It was awful. I can imagine others being taken in by the flash touch screen, which was not all that easy to navigate, and that they could feed it 6 CDs and do a mix, but not us. We later compared it on our 14 year old car and our old Millennium Ford Focus did a much better job. It wasn’t irritating and my wife started singing along to certain prized listens.

Hmmmm!? I just thought, I should have thrown in a sea chantey recording too. My wife loses herself on such things. On this trip it was Nanci Griffith, Michael Bublé, and, of course, Cerys Matthews and Catatonia. We particularly like “Mulder & Scully” and “Road Rage”… The Škoda sound system is so bad she just made faces and complained, and in a live recording you couldn’t make sense of the what the audience sang on the chorus to Bublé‘s “Haven’t Met You Yet”, while in the Focus it was clear. All other cars we’ve tested have the main speaker forward on the doors, where the sound is allowed to be ’open’, and all had tweeters too. The Škoda sound was not just tinny, it was boxy too, not rich, not full ~ awful!

So, is the sound system in the car we might purchase important ~ DAMN RIGHT IT IS!!! One whole make of auto has consequently been stricken off our considerations. While, a bit worried about this, one has been added, as ~ a DYNAUDIO 10 speaker system is one of their options… :-) ;-) We might test drive that today, but we have scheduled to do so on Wednesday…

Dumb Sound by Volkswagen (PLC = VW / Audi / Skoda / etc., etc., etc…) Volkswagen SportsVan

Some things are just too idiotic to believe. ALL Volkswagen Golfs, including the upcoming (but yet to be named for Britain) Golf SportsVan, and the Audi A3, have their single CD player tucked up at the top inside the glove compartment / glovebox! :-/ Why? It’s not easily accessible from the driver’s position, definitely NOT while driving, and the passenger would need to open and close the compartment for every change of a CD, or leave it open. Entering and removing a CD is no easy thing. And, you’d also want to be careful about what you kept in that compartment.

What a shame! How dumb can one get? And the Dynaudio system available with the Volkswagon Golf models is so sweet, we loved it, but the placement of the CD player is so idiotic, we hate that. Our hearts dropped after being showed where the CD player was. We were very interested in this new and larger and more spacious and flexible Volkswagen Golf SportsVan, due out in Britain later this year. That interest is gone, POOF!!! We’ve completely written off any consideration for a Volkswagen Golf of any variety, simply because of where some clueless design guru has decided to hide the CD player. The exact same setup is found in the Audi A3 models, also a VW company. Dumb! Dumb!! DUMB!!!

http://www.volkswagen.co.uk/about-us/news/548

http://www.autoblog.com/2013/09/10/2014-volkswagen-golf-sportsvan-concept-frankfurt-2013/

But, already mentioned, and even more ridiculous, the placement of speakers on the Skodas, in particular the Yeti, also a VW company ~ :-P The main speakers are tucked in the back of the doors, below and aimed into the driver’s and the passenger’s seats, and the tweeters are at the top of the door, below the window, aimed at the driver and passenger. WHY? How clueless can one get about sound? I have seen similar madness with how some crazies have set up live sound situations, or sound for halls and pubs. Why not ask someone who might know for a little guidance in such things as the placement of speakers? It should be ‘common sense’! But, obviously from situations like these, ‘common sense’ sometimes takes a vacation, or is missing completely.

We had one person, a completely different dealership, ask us “Why is the sound so important to you?” In this case we were test driving Nissans, with a high interest in the latest Qashqai. While we like it, there are reservations about the sound, and there’s no option for any kind of upgrade. The Nissans have a two speaker system in the front, and the placement and balance between the two isn’t bad, and the highs have some crispness, though they could be better defined. BUT, the main speakers are muddy and the vocals get lost, blended with the instrumentals, poor separation. We still like the Qashqai. It is a contender, but, they’ve cut corners with the sound and the speakers that come installed are clearly not great quality. While my wife loves the higher ride, the actual on the road feel, for me, was a bit mushy, but I do realize that is also probably due to that higher ride, build.

Still furrowing brows, but we have had some lovely rides, and we’ve heard some decent sound systems, which included the Volkswaged Golf and Golf SportsVan option of Dynaudio, but a shame about the placement of the CD player… :-P SMH
