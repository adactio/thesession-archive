Jeremy: “So have at it, everyone: let me know what features you’d like to see on the site…”

@Jeremy: Here goes!

1) Not all hornpipes are played “swung” (i.e. dotted). When submitting a tune with a hornpipe classification, you can of course transcribe your ABC submission to reflect this. In the case of “unswung” hornpipes, the sheet music derived from the ABC is correct , whereas the midi rendering is not. Yes, I know that you don’t much care for midi but very many of us find it useful. Could you please, please fix this bug? Thanks!

2) I like the relatively new feature whereby you get a preview of the sheet music prior to the confirmation of a tune submission. However, this preview doesn’t include the key signature (if there is one). A relatively minor bug, but maybe one worth fixing.

3) I really like the “notifications” feature that you introduced a while back. It’s a pity though that it’s limited to submissions where you happen to be the originator. For example, if you post an alternate setting of a tune, and someone subsequently comments on your setting, you wouldn’t receive a notification.

Keep up the good work!

Mix
