↳ Posted by TheBlindBard on November 17th, 2013.

Re: Tunes keeping their own time?

BB hammer-ons and pull-offs are fine, as long as they don’t disrupt the rhythm.A lot of beginners tend to rush the slurs(hammers or pulls) so that instead of playing, for example A2B2 you end up with AB3. Your question however unintentionally opens up a can of worms. The way I see it, hammers or pulls i.e. slurs from one note to another are types of articulation but not ornamentation. Ornamentation generally invoves adding extra notes to the tune,played so quickly that they aren’t counted in the timing and don’t disrupt the timing of the tune. One exception to this is triplets,which involves playing three notes where there was either one long note(quarter note) or two short notes(eight notes). The additional notes do have rhythmic values but once again do not change the number of beats in the measure. The controversy comes because many people ,myself included think of ornaments as a type of articulation,although separate from slurring notes of the tune. Others here vehemently disagree. If you check out Father Ryan Duns’ youtube tutorials for whistle you will find he explains and demonstrates basic ornamentation for Irish trad very well.http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-1CQqbT0bIw is the 4th lesson where he starts to teach ornamentation.
