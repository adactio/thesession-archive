This takes the biscuit. Plus a quiz question.

So. I went along to the Upton on Severn Folk Festival this evening. I wouldn’t have bothered, but I’d done a gig there the other day, and that gave me free passes for the weekend. This evening was the first time I’d ventured back. Never been to this particular folk festival before. (Never will again, but that’s another story …)

Couldn’t see anything I fancied, so I ended up in (I think it’s called) The Swan at the far end of town by the river. Looked like a nice session, there was one free chair so I asked the people in the session if it was free - it was - and was in the act of sitting down. I took a little time to do so, ’cos I was getting my instrument off my back first. Big mistake.

This woman plonked an “instrument” on the chair, claiming that that chair was now “hers”. I “explained” to her (ahem) and she was about to leave me to it. And then I thought “D’ya know, I just can’t be *rsed”, so I went home again. No tunes for me tonight. I’d heard a few of the fellas, and met them previously - nice players, and I was really looking forward to it. Some people just don’t get it - by which, I don’t mean session etiquette or anything like that - I mean being decently human.

Anyway: quiz question: what was the “instrument” she plonked on the chair?
