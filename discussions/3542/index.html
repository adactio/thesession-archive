A laugh for Jim

Nobody should go through the day without several laughs, so Jim, here’s one that made me laugh when my friend e-mailed it to me on Monday, from off a etiquette posting board she frequents:

"This is a story my mom is fond of recounting. When my father was a geology graduate student, my mother and father were invited to a professor’s house. There were several couples there (mostly composed of various graduate students and their dates). The professor in question had two very large, very beautiful geodes on display. For those of you who don’t know, geodes are large noules that you crack open to show the hundreds to thousands of crystals that grow on the inside. In this case, the geodes were three to four feet big and had beautiful amethyst crystals on the inside.

One girl, approached the professor to compliment him on them. “My, you have a lovely set of gonads.”

Dead silence from everyone around her. Without missing a beat, the professor responded with “Thank you. I hear that all the time.” He then quickly changed the topic."

Anybody else got a good laugh for Jim? (And, coincidentally, the rest of us?)

“We had a wonderful dinner the other evening at a new restaurant”, one said.
