Re: Whos your favourite fiddler player… and why?

I don’t think that Yhaalhouse is playing the troll, I believe that he is perfectly serious. The “pointless masturbation” quote is getting all the attention but really his key point is this:

<<This is an aural tradition. It requires passing from person to person in the now. Get into a pub’ with a session and make your own arrangements with the tunes not sit listening to some hackneyed old shight or some re-heated up old bollox in your bedroom. That’s killing the tradition.>>

The facts of the matter are that it is hasn’t been a purely aural tradition for a long, long time. The older players that we hold in high regard would learn tunes from 78’s, from the radio and from the dots if they could read music. But mostly they learned tunes from one another as we continue to do today. Far from killing the tradition the wide availability of top-quality recorded material is one of the factors that is keeping it vibrant not just in Ireland but as a global phenomenon.

PS In the above I talk about passing on tunes but obviously when you learn from another player or a recording then you can assimiliate a whole lot more than just the notes.
