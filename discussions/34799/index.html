What are the darkest, gloomiest tunes you know?

It’s all in the discussion title. I’m giving for granted we’re talking about minor-key tunes, especially reels. What are the darkest, blackest, or perhaps angriest reels you know of? I believe Dm/Ddor is the darkest key. A leering key, every fear, every hesitation of a lamentful heart breathes out of horrible D minor. Therefore I’d go with Master Crowley’s, Porthole in the Kelp and the Tamlin reel.
