New Dry Tuned Piano Accordion

I’ve just recently played a new 72 bass Exclesior piano accordion which has been dry tuned from the factory and I think it’s possibly the best box so far that I’ve played. I’ve had/got a Saltarelle, Pigini and Vignoni and I think this is at least as good if not better and a much better price! Anyone else tried one?

The new exclesiors are made in Italy by Pigini and have all the usual spec like wooden keyboards and Dural reeds as standard…very nice!

Re: New Dry Tuned Piano Accordion

I didn’t expect such interest in the post…first of all it wasn’t meant as an advert, if so I would have used an anonymous username and posted links to the Hobgoblin site and you tube clips with prices etc…

It was a question about what Accordion players think (as I’m one myself) of dry tuned boxes that they’ve played. It’s far too easy for people to get stuck with a ‘normal’ tuned Accordion with a wide musette which you could be in danger of geting evicted from a session with or over priced, over hyped instruments which I have purchased in error myself over the years.

The different Hobgoblin shops have various areas of expertise depending on staff interests. The Birmingham shop includes myself and a flute/whistle player who have played Irish trad all over the place for many years in sessions and gigs, it’s what we love!

We give free info out to people every week who either have an instrument they want to sell privately, just need a valuation or background history. We are an instrument retailer but are also there to help promote all types of traditional music playing including puttng people in touch with this site for tunes etc..

Sorry if some people may have got the wrong impression about the original post
