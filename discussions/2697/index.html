Sorry TdB (Ta-da-Boom?), but you might get a more helpful reply if you give us more to go on, including something about yourself in your member bio.

Irish trad music is played by a variety of instruments, among them fiddle, flute, whistle, uillean pipes, concertina, button and piano accordion, bouzouki, guitar, piano, harp, and shakey egg. Some tunes are more suited to certain instruments, likely because they were created on that instrument, and some tunes go below the normal range for, say, a D whistle or flute, but in most cases there are ways around that.

Oh, and sheet music is not a primary vector for spreading this disease--it’s much more contagious aurally. Generally, the dots are a memory aid at best, and a mutated strain at worst.
