Do You Have Enough Whimsy in Your Life

Sorry for such a strange question, but I felt a sudden “whimsical” urge to ask it.

Personally, I think this music, in spirit, contains a fair amount of whimsy, and at least some whimsy in one’s heart to play it, but sadly I find it woefully deficient on the mustard boards. Even yhaalhouse’s wig glue and hot water bottle collection cannot quell the rampant curmudgeonry here, for feck’s sake.

A philosophical (or anti-philosophical depending on how you look at it) question. Responses containing whimsy will be fondly considered.

Signed,
Captain Underpants
