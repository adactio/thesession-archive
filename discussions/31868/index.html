Re: Lambeg vs the bodhran

Leaving aside the politics, the ax grinders and the nonsensical posts (I mean, do you alter your opinion of a guitar because of the music it plays?) there are many different rhythms played on lambeg drums, and many areas have their own regional favourites as well as variations. There are drumming competitions and some of the rhythms even have chants to be sung alongside the beat. “Down down down the Moneymore road” is one I remember from my childhood. These have been handed down for generations, while new compositions appear all the time, just as in any form of local music. There’s much more to them than a thrash at an Orange procession. I’ve played both bodhran and lambeg - in a totally non-political environment I may add - and there is real skill involved.
