Re: going to sessions are like going to mass!

I see the similarities between a satisfying religious service and a satisfying session. The feeling of oneness with others, the warmth, the feeling of trancendence that participating in music brings. Yet there is a difference, perhaps understood only by those who are rooted in a faith.
The Church’s ban on secular music in services, which I find is only loosely enforced in my neck of the woods, is intended to keep the service rooted in the sacred. Yet I know many a secular tune that has been turned into a hymn, and many a hymn’s tune has travelled into the secular world. Hard to draw lines.
My own Sundays often start with church and end with a session, which makes it a very special day.
