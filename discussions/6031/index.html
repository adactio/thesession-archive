…And the night was ruined (Part 2!)

I play in a well known Irish music bar in Dublin’s city centre.
It is usually great fun, and there is a wide range of musicians.
Last night there was a Japanese and a German playing Irish music and playing beautifully.

As we all know, there were a couple of big football matches on last night, so thePub was filled with punters high in spirit and maybe a little tipsy!

Anyway after a while of us playing there were three individuals
Stood at the bar right in front of us who seemed quite oblivious to the fact that we were playing the tunes – one in particular.

They shouted loudly over the music, and banged into people etc.
It is important to note that there was plenty of room in the rest of the pub. In this particular pub there are often tourists – actually, there are always tourists inside.

And to hear these Guys talk loudly was actually embarrassing, and cringeworthy. They were crude, loud, brash and downright annoying, and being a Dubliner myself, It made it all the more enfuriating to hear these ambassadors of our country talk….….”ROIGHT?”

After a while, one punter, asked them to keep it down, as he was trying to enjoy the music.Then another, and then another.

The resident musician finally stood up and yelled – “Would you please shut up or go into the backroom, or go out to the smoking area”

He was answered with;
”It’s a pub – not a fu**in library”
”Why don’t you play your music somewhere else, It all sounds the same anyway”
”I paid €4 for my pint, and Im staying here…”

After this, the music stopped, the musicians went home all a little dejected and a little shocked!

Now its important to state that this guy was not too drunk but tipsy, and that music has been played in this bar for 40 years on a nightly basis in the same spot.

I couldn’t believe it, and felt like smacking the guy.

Ok that’s off my chest, but would love to hear what others have to say, have they experience something similar, or do they have advice?

Re: …And the night was ruined (Part 2!)

We regularly have Welsh, Irish, and other rugby supporters in Edinburgh and they will frequently take over pubs (even known session venues). The management take the view that this is a “one off” and we should be welcoming visitors. This is true, of course, and it’s probably to easy to dismiss these publicans etc as being greedy. They have a living to make so it’s only sensible to take advantage of these opportunities. However, there is a case that the welcome should be “on our terms”. If I go to a bar or restaurant elsewhere, I can judge what type of establishment it is and behave accordingly. I wouldn’t enter the cocktail lounge or whatever of The Ritz or go into McDonald’s(well I wouldn’t go there anyway) and start playing tunes. Yet, there’s no reason why these or any other premises shouldn’t be as busy as possible with their own crowd.
