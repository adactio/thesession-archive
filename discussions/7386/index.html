Pedantry: discuss

To the tune of Thank God We’re Surrounded by Water:

Come all ye lovers of music and song
And take heed of my warning, it will not take long,
Of a well known annoying phenomenon
It’s known as the musical pedant
With logic to rival that of Mr Spock
He’ll correct and hold forth all the way round the clock
In that cakehole of his I wish he’d put a sock
The creature that’s known as the pedant

With notebook, thesaurus, WAP phone and a pen
He’ll appear at your session, he’ll take notes and then
With his fine eye for detail drive you round the bend
Disputing your tunes, chords and rhythm
Even with his love interests he’ll be pedantic
With female philologists he’ll get romantic
And argue the toss on matters semantic
Ah sure he’s naught but a pedant

At Belfast Folk Festival he made an appearance
The organisers oblivious to his interference
Made flyers and posters without asking for clearance
My God, they should have known better
He murmured “This poster reads strangely to me;
Melarkey containing a multiple “e”
Is not what you’ll find in the new OED
Has Patsy not heard of the deed poll?”

To a session of songs our friend did repair
He was very soon tearing at his head of hair
Unhappy was he with the musical fare.
Yangon is the capital of Myanmar
Once Maighreád Ni Mhaonaigh had sung a fine song
He bellowed “your pronunciation’s all wrong”
And the original version is at least twice as long
Dolphins use echolocation

His comments on etiquette, decency and form
And contempt of those who dare to stray from the norm
Could in some circumstances kick up quite a storm
If you give his words any importance
His pursuit of perfection and truth with a hunger
That betrays his delusion he is Felix Ungar
Can only suggest he’s not getting any younger
He has far too much time on his hands

And so to conclude this cautionary tale
Beware of those sticklers who feign would assail
You with exhaustive words and intellectual blackmail
They’re not worth the steam off your p**s
If you want to play music, sing songs, fart or yodel
Who cares if it’s major or minor or modal
I’m not bothered if Quantum physics is nodal
Stay clear of the musical pedant
