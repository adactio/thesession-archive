Recordings of the “Session of the Century”

Over the last few months I did share some rare recordings from the Willie Clancy festival. None of those recordings belongs to any commercial release and it’s not the average stuff you can buy on iTunes or in a store. In my opinion those recordings deserve an audience and should serve educational purposes. I also wanted people to help me with identifying the tunes from those tapes. (I am referring to this old thread : https://thesession.org/discussions/33006). KidOnTheMountain did an impressive identification work but he did not identify tunes from the 3 recordings below !

I guess people were a little bit scared to download the mp3 from my DropBox because of viruses so I joined all the mp3 together and put them on my Soundcloud account. People can now listen them directly on the Internet without downloading them :

https://soundcloud.com/ralexhassle/session-of-the-century-1

https://soundcloud.com/ralexhassle/session-of-the-century-2

https://soundcloud.com/ralexhassle/session-of-the-century-3

I would be extremly glad if some poeple could help me with identifying the tunes. Soundcloud allows listeners to leave comments at specific points in the waveform.

Enjoy,
Ralex

Re: Recordings of the “Session of the Century”

I was at Track 2. Queally’s bar in 1985 in the afternoon. At, not in, because the place was so packed that we couldn’t get in and had to listen standing in the street. My mate taped it through the window but this seems to come from someone else’s recording. It sounds considerably speeded up to me. (They were playing in Eb.) The core of the session was Jackie Daly, Mairead and Frankie, Paul O‘Shaughnessy, Eoin O’Neill on bouzouki and Mick O’Connor on banjo. Mary Bergin was there for bits of it. Later on in the afternoon most of them spilled across the road into Cleary’s kitchen where they were joined by Dermy Diamond and Henry Benagh - we were able to get in to that one!
