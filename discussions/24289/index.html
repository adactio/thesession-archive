Re: Is this the future

When I was first introduced to Irish trad music, I was a long-time club DJ, and had done a number of industrial compositions, and commercially-released re-mixes of other people’s music.

One of the things that first drew me to Irish was the similarities of the music with some electronic music (intricate melodies that were very catchy, but repetitive… Lots of energy., etc.) And my first inclination was to do some electronic versions of Irish tunes. (Which were way better than that one, BTW). But it couldn’t have been more than 6 months into it that I was starting to understand the nuances of the music that was completely ruined by electronic versions of it. So those ideas, thankfully, died right then and there.

I gave up the DJ (and nightclub owner) lifestyle that I had lived for so many years about the same time that I started playing Irish. And I became so enamored with the Irish music, that it has probably been 7-8 years since I even really listened to anything else. Rock, electronic, industrial, goth, and all the stuff that I was so into for so much of my life, generally bores me after about a minute of listening to it anymore. But I can listen to Irish all day. :-D
