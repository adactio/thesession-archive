Re: Syncopated foot tapping definitely a modern phenomenon

Martin typically gets the downbeat on one foot and the backbeat on the other, and then switches back and forth. Which seems very apt for this music, where the emphasis is always moving around.

But I wonder if that’s what deeor is asking about.

Not sure if an old clip of a ceili band is where to look, though, for evidence of past trends on this. Ceili bands almost always have a drummer, and the players are focused a fair amount on staying tight together. Less of a need to tap your foot at all, and it may even be a distraction. Some players who would tap at a session or when playing solo might not tap at all in a ceili band situation.

Also, in formal settings, past generations may have been more subdued than today’s players.
