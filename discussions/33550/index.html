Re: A cautionary tale

Let me revisit why I think this song fails and comes across as childish. In essence, the song says this: Man joins session, plays spoons, is annoying, so someone kills him. Ha Ha. I guess to someone who hates spoons or percussion in general, the obvious solution comes across as funny, but only because the victim supposedly got his just deserts, not because there was a good set-up and payoff to the “joke.”

Let me try an old joke I know in a similar vein and see if you can see the difference:

A rather slight fellow is sitting in a truck stop having his lunch. Three huge bikers come in and converge around the fellow’s table and proceed to mock his size, his clothes, his long hair and his taste in food. They poor ketchup over his head, mix his meal into his cup of coffee and generally humiliate him. Finally, and without a word, the poor sod puts money on the table for his food and walks out. The bikers guffaw and yell at the waiter, “Well, it seems that young fella don’t know how to take a joke!” Gazing out the window, the waiter replies, “Looks like he doesn’t know how to drive, either. He just ran his 18-wheeler over three Harleys on his way out of here.”
