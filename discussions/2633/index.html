The Reforging Of The Mighty Craic Album

Hello Everybody,

A month or two ago, as I was looking through the discussion archives, I found this discussion about something called the “Mighty Craic Album.” I read the whole thing and found out that it was an album in the making with one track recorded by each of the session members that wanted to contribute. Anyone who recorded a track for it would then receive a copy of the album.

I thought, “Wow, what an awesome idea!” and did a search for other discussions on the topic. I found one that was asking why it had never happened. I wrote to Zina and asked the same question. She said that people had been working on it and a lot of people had sent in tracks, but production had come to a standstill. I asked her if we could try to get it started again, because it really is an awesome idea. She told me to wait until after Christmas. I did, and I just wrote to her recently asking if it really was a possibility. She said:

“…the first thing is to find somebody who would be willing to collect all the recordings and re-master them so they sound more or less the same sort of quality overall. That someone will have to have time, equipment, and know-how in order for this to come out even remotely something any of us would play more than once!”

Is there anyone out there willing to do this? I understand that this is a huge job, and if this does get started again, I would be glad to do anything I could to help. Unfortunatly, I don’t have the equipment and know-how to re-master all the tracks, so I woudn’t be able to do that.

What I’m really getting at is this: is there still interest in such a project or would it just flop again? It sounds really fun, so let me know if you are all still interested, or if you would be able to help with the production process.

Thanks!

-Max

The Reforging Of The Mighty Craic Album Begins!

Well, okay, lads, here we go…

Max is going to collect the stuff, we think, stuff to work out there; Tusong has generously volunteered to master the things and put ’em into MP3 format (the very biggest job of all), and I will take care of the packaging, replication and such.

Once we get closer, I’ll be asking people who want a copy to send me a spot o’cash by Pay Pal or such to pay for the shipping and physical CD. So stand by on sending everything, more news shall appear here as it comes available.

The point of the CD is to share our music with each other, not to stun the Irish traditional music world with our virtuousity, so everything from a tape recording in your living room/local session to a full blown studio track is welcome. However, these are the stipulations:

1) All tracks, sets, and arrangements must be original to you -- no doing a Bothy Band set or Michael Coleman or Lunasa or for that matter, anyone else but you, etc. Please do not send something off your band’s CD, though a track of your band (so long as #2 is followed) is fine. A copyright infringement suit is not *my* idea of a fun time.
2) The track must be of you, not of a huge session where we can’t tell which instrument is you.
3) Doesn’t matter what format, Tusong says he can handle them all.
4) Please be prepared to send your track by March 31 of this year. (Hopefully that way everyone can get their St. Pat’s commitments out of the way, and/or record themselves performing said commitment if they so wish.)

In the meantime, I shall go start a “dibsing” thread. No hogging a bunch of tunes “just in case”, although one or two is okay to hedge your bets. You may also change your mind so long as someone has not taken the tune in the meantime. Please keep discussions of the album OFF that thread, it’ll just be for tune dibs, discuss any issues here rather than there.

(For you more experienced players, it would be *kind* to leave the more familiar tunes -- Sligo Maid, Maid Behind the Bar, etc. -- to those for whom they make up their entire repertoire. But if you have to, you have to.)

I’ll let you guys know about where to send the tracks soon.

Zina

Re: copyright questions -- if you want to do a tune with a known composer (ie: Liz Carroll or Jay Unger) and that composer is still living, before you give up the idea of recording it for this, WRITE (e-mail) THEM AND ASK IF YOU MAY RECORD IT FOR THIS. First, they can only say no. Second, they will be flattered. Third, they will be exceedingly grateful that you bothered to ask. Just explain the project and say that you’d like to use their tune “Not a Gan Ainm” and would that be all right with them? 8 times out of 10, they’ll say yes.

You can reach Liz Carroll, Jay Ungar, and lots of the other artists through their websites. (Liz Carroll, the only one on your list I’ve personally had contact with, Rog, is one of the nicest women alive, and I can’t really see her saying no, though I suppose it’s possible.)

Anyway, if y’all will do this, it will save me the time of doing it myself and besides which it never hurts you to have contact with those we consider the leaders of our community.
