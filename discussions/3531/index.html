There are certain tunes which I hear all the time at sessions because, for some reason unknown to me, people insist on playing them. One particular example is “King of the Fairies” which is particularly irritating. Do any of you have tunes like that?

I’ve played with a lot of different musicians over the years and I’m always amazed how the same tune one of them finds revolting is often the very tune that is the other’s favorite. Also, some people will dislike the simple repetitive tunes, but others will favor them over the more circuitous ones. Most of the time negative associations are what ruins a tune for folks it seems, or just bad first impressions because of the performance of it at the time. I admit to having one or two tunes that I might sit out, or join in with only out of politeness, but I also realize that my negative feelings about the tune are possibly biased, and I try to keep an open mind. There has been more than one occasion where a tune I didn’t favor much transmogrified into a tune I really liked. For this reason I try to learn all the tunes my cohorts play -- even if I’m not that keen on them.
