Jackie has a story about playing a gig somewhere in Cork, so it would have included lots of polkas. Afterwards, a man came up said, “Mr. Daly, you’re good, but my daughter can play twice as fast.”

Lots of great recordings of polkas. Too many to give links to the recordings. Johnny O’Leary, Jackie Daly (the Music from Sliabh Luachra album and the one with Seamus Creagh), Breandan Begley, Dan Herlihy & John Drew come to mind.

I love the few examples from Clare musicians that I’ve heard both on commercial recordings and live, but it’s almost a different thing entirely.

Back around September or early October, shortly after I arrived at the session, wearing a warm sweater since the weather was starting to get a little cool, we sang New York Girls, inserting a polka or two in the middle to allow a couple of people to dance. The tempo was fairly brisk and they danced longer than usual. By the end, I was sweating and hot, so I took my sweater off. One of the other musicians wanted to play more polkas! I said if we played a few more, I’d be naked, suddenly realizing that we would be playing strip polka.

Re: Polkas

The clip was on the fast side, but seemed OK to me. Also, I don’t think we should get too precious about polkas. They’ve always seemed to me to be a bit ‘outside’ the rest of the tradition, a bit … well ,,, foreign.

I mean, if you take a good jig or reel, even if it’s a new one, it will be part of a tradition of jigs and reels which stretches back, unbroken as far as I know, over a thousand years. Polkas are, again as far as I know, ‘new’ in the sense that they developed and became popular in the late 19th century, and then REALLY took off in the mid 20th century, with the polka bands. Now, I’ll admit that I’m saying this from memory without looking it up, so I’d be glad to be corrected if I’m wrong …

I’m not necessarily trying to be disparaging about polkas, although personally I could do without them. But, partly, what I’m saying is that I think they’re still developing as a form - 100 years or so isn’t enough - so it’s too soon to be prescriptive about things like speed.
