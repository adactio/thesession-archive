The Daisy Rock range with butterflies, hearts and daisy shaped guitars looks fantastic!
Is this trend only for the rock scene? Any other instruments needing a bit of “girliefication”- bagpipes? fiddles, whistles?

Re: Guitars for girls?

I was listening to a live radio concert featuring a female singer-songwriter from Eastern Massachusetts (sorry, can’t recall the name), during which she started talking about her guitar, an acoustic model with built-in electronic pick-up. She explained that when she first started using it, she noticed something wrong with the sound; the volume kept rising and falling, very erratically.
Finally, she discovered the problem: Her breast kept rubbing against the volume control button, which was located on the side of the guitar, near the neck. So, she took the guitar into the shop so as to have the button relocated.

Now, it’s one thing to explain such a situation to your friendly neighborhood luthier. But to a whole radio audience? Wow, more than I ever thought I’d hear from a complete stranger.

Serious reply……….
About three years ago my teenage daughter, having already adopted my japanese acoustic guitar, found an electric guitar neck in my junk box, that I’d picked up at the local charity shop for £2.00, thinking I might find a use for it someday.
I was persuaded to add on the rest of the neccessary pices to make this a functioning instrument (cost a bit more than two quid too…) and gave her the option of designing body and pickguard shapes herself, plus letting her do a lot of the finishing as well.
It’s definitely a girls’ guitar, boys look at it and go, it’s a guitar,but girls like the curved shape she designed for it, and do enjoy looking at it and handling it more than the lads.
So maybe the designers need to bring in a woman to design girl-friendly instruments, not just stick a flower on it, or make it in pink.
