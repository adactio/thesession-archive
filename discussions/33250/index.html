Worthington, I have no desire to crash your thread, but (a) your question has been pretty well answered, and (b) I don’t want you to feel unduly upset and/or believe that anybody thinks the less of you for what you quite rightly point out is a trivial mistake that can be easily made by anybody. Another is that despite the house rules it’s inevitable that threads will wander off on all sorts of tangents (often to the point of ridiculousness), and yet another characteristic of this community is that although it is rich with experienced people with good serious advice, it is largely fuelled by good natured banter. Your particular slip (and we know that that’s all it was) just couldn’t go unchallenged, purely on the basis that it’s one of the many pet peeves of many people on this site.
