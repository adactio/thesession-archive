Should session attendees be encouraged to get vaccinated?

This question may open up a can of worms, but I’m sure it is bound to be discussed as sessions are opening up. Here in the States there has been some resistance to getting vaccinated for the coronavirus. Some may have a legitimate reason for this, which I cannot argue with, however my observation is that many are simply swayed by whatever political party they are affiliated with, or whichever information outlet they favor. So, should musicians at the session who have been vaccinated be concerned regarding the attendance of a musician who is just not interested in getting vaccinated? More to the point, should any musician be excluded from a session for willingly not getting a vaccination? Obviously, this brings up a certain policing element, which is not foreign to ITM sessions (i.e. session etiquette).

If the vaccines work, a few unvaccinated folks showing up won’t matter. The vaccinated are not at risk.

I don’t know if the law would cut the establishment any slack because they delegate the task to a non-employee session host to do it, but I wouldn’t count on it.

"In a sharp turnabout from previous recommendations, federal health officials on Thursday advised that Americans who are fully vaccinated against the coronavirus may stop wearing masks or maintaining social distance in most indoor and outdoor settings, regardless of size.

The new advice comes with caveats. Even vaccinated individuals must cover their faces and physically distance when going to doctors, hospitals or long-term care facilities like nursing homes; when traveling by bus, plane, train or other modes of public transportation, or while in transportation hubs like airports and bus stations; and when in prisons, jails or homeless shelters."

The new advisory apparently allows mingling indoors with non-vaccinated people. It’s not a Federal mandate; individual States can still make their own regulations. My current state doesn’t allow this, but I’d expect it to follow the CDC recommendation within a month or two.

This part of the article below is interesting, about how allowing people to go un-masked indoors may incentivize more people to get vaccinated:

"Allowing immunized people to go mask-free indoors may help persuade more people to opt for the vaccine, said Angela Rasmussen, a virologist at Vaccine and Infectious Diseases Organization in Saskatchewan, Canada. Removing mask requirements “is another incentive that is extremely low cost and is very much supported by evidence.”
