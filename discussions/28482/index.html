I think that one of the defining differences between Scottish and Irish music is that, traditionally, Irish music has been passed around in more sloppy manner, which suits most pub sessions. I’m not condoning this, it’s just the way it is.

The vast majority of Irish pub sessions around the world are groups of not very good players faffing along with slightly different versions of tunes that kind of fit together in a kind of low brow fun way and fair play to it .. it beats staying home and watching pop idol. But I think that Scottish music requires a little more precision (I think Irish music requires more precision also, of course, but it doesn’t get it so that’s by the by). If you think of these bit six part Scottish pipe tunes for example, to your average Irish musician trying to pic up a tune like that in the pub, it’s just really a two part tune with some variations, if that.
