Re: Old-time fiddle w/piano

I liked the fiddling in the first two clips - lots of good rhythm and drive there. It’s quite clear that Calvin Volraith is by far the better fiddler :)

As for the pianos in both, I personally don’t like them (or piano accompaniment in general, unless it’s someone of the class of Paul Machlis), but that’s just me.

The last clip where Calvin Vollraith is playing an octave violin - that can be either a 4/4 violin body, or a 14 1/2" viola body, strung EADG (high-to-low), but a full octave below normal violin pitch.

I’ve played few of them, and they all had a good tone. They do take a bit of getting used to, mainly because of the slower response from the thicker strings. They would normally require a heavier bow, too.

Re: Old-time fiddle w/piano

Hello, so sorry for arriving late for the party. I just discovered this discussion today and thought I’d chirp in. I’m a fan of many styles of fiddling and as I’ve never heard Anna play fiddle before, I really like her fiddling in the video. She plays great. Just to clarify, I’m not a classical player by any means. I play by ear. I grew up playing Canadian old time fiddle, which is slightly different than American old time fiddling. Our most famous old time fiddler in Canada was Don Messer. Tunes like Red Wing, Ragtime Annie, Big John McNeil, St. Anne’s Reel, Fisher’s Hornpipe (but played more like a reel) and the list goes on and on.

At the age of 21, I joined up with a country band and learned about backup fiddle. Totally different than playing fiddle tunes but in time I got the hack of it, kinda and got exposed to many different styles of music and tried my hand at making my fiddle fit into each style. I never got great at 1 one of them but was never afraid to step in and try. I learned it was great fun to open my mind and enjoy other styles. Because of this, I’ve been able to make a living playing fiddle for 45 years now.

I’m a huge fan of Kevin Burke in the Irish style, Bruce Molsky in the old time style, Johnny Gimble in the western swing style, Andre Brunet in the French Canadian style, Aly Bain in the Scottish style, Tim Crouch in the bluegrass style, Andy DeJarlis in the Canadian old time style and the list can go on and on.

I don’t expect everyone to enjoy my fiddling. That’s ok. There’s some fiddlers out there that I don’t enjoy the music they make either but I wouldn’t slander their playing. Just because I might not like it, don’t mean that it’s bad.

Once again, I love Anna’s fiddling and I will be googling her and looking for more videos.
Whoever posted the video of me playing, thank you for introducing me to this website.
