Ah Saint, since you’ve entered this forum you have made some very interesting contributions and long may it continue. But back to the question, ‘good person V good musician’. What is ‘good person’? Who am I to judge who is ‘good’? You are entering theological, psychological and philosophical holy ground there and far be it for me to expound on the essence of goodness and who is to be allocated the distinction of being a ‘good person’. There are many books on the subject and the following link may be of some help. http://books.google.ie/books?q=define+good+person&ots=KebJI9SNwx&sa=X&oi=print&ct=title
Now a good musician on the other hand, although somewhat subjective, is easier to identify. Personally I would not like to play in a group consisting solely of inept musicians although a session of ‘good’ musicians with a few musicians at the learning stage especially when they show both genuine interest and progression is great. IMO anyone entering the session scene should be encouraged by the better musicians and any criticism should be constructive and extremely tactfully given. A beginner generally knows well that they are inadequate and are utlra-sensitive to any criticism. In fact it is my experience that many ‘good musicians’ are very often dissatisfied with their own playing standard. ‘Good musician V good person’ can we not have good musician who is a reasonable person, I’d be happy with that.

This is my short reply. Wanna see the long winded version? :)
