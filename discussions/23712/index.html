↳ Posted by Piece on February 2nd, 2010.

Re: When is it OK to use a synthesizer in Irish music?

Hi farmerboy. It’s flogging a dead horse for us on here with the whole instrument hierarchy articulation thing.

You know how a tune deedles? Well, whistles, pipes and flutes deedle. Fiddles scrape, but they mimic deedles. They can mimic anything. Boxes, banjos and mandos don’t deedle. They honk or plunk. It’s just musical physics. This music has specific articulations that are natural for those instruments. The rest of us have to approximate them.

It’s not “Oh, the pipes are better than the box just because” it’s an actual scientific thing going on there, if you think about it.

More: https://thesession.org/discussions/6109
