Added by Jerone on May 3rd, 2011

Frustrated! Searching for “The Stranger Tune”.

One of the hardest things about this lifestyle, is the constant search for the music I’ve heard in the past. What I’m frustrated about now, is i can’t find a tune that I’ve heard recently.

I heard a tune at work. Cracker Barrel Old Country Store is the name of the place. It plays a lot of Old-Time, Appalachian, Bluegrass, and Country music.

Sometimes i get the chance to hear the tune. It sounds like an Air. It’s droned all the way through. Beautiful piece, short and sweet. It doesn’t sound hard, and i wanna learn it. So the desperate search begins.

I talked to my manager today to try and find out what it was. He said there’s no way to find out because our stores’ music is pipped in through a private satellite radio channel. He said the only thing i can do is to call home office.

So i called, and they said that the next time it plays, to write down the date and time, and the song that comes on afterwards. Hopefully this works, now i just need to wait until the next day i work and hope that i hear it. I may just go up and sit in for several hours hoping it plays, which will be very stressful.

There is only one song/piece, that i’ve ever heard, that i have not yet found again. It was some simple piano melody from a commercial that i fail to remember.

So has anyone ever been through the “Desperate Search” for “The Stranger Tune”(just made that up). Is there a way to find a tune with virtually no resources or prior knowledge?

Re: Frustrated! Searching for “The Stranger Tune”.

Fiddlelearner I had a similar “desperate search” Years ago when “The Thistle and Shamrock” was a new program I heard a beautiful few verses of a song on the program by a male singer. The doorbell rang and I never heard the end of the tune. I did catch the name of the artist, or so I thought. The name was Dougie Maclean. Well I searched for Dougie Maclean in record stores with no luck. It was the mid to late 80’s. Finally with the advent of the internet I was able to do more research and I started to find a cd now and then. Still not the the song I was looking for but I enjoyed Dougie’s music a great deal. I heard the Tribute cd and Niel Gow’s Lament to the death of his 2nd wife" and I knew I had to learn the fiddle. I had played classical viola so it wasn’t a stretch. But that search has led me to this site, and workshops down at Swannanoa and lessons and competitions in the traditional Scottish fiddle world. I have every one of Dougie’s cd’s and the mystery tune is not on a single cd, but the search has led me to new friends and opportunities. I’ve been playing for 5 years now and I have dozens of Scottish and Irish tunes in my repetoire because of my obsession with one mystery tune.
