Navigator/Reveal Records Mixtape - free download

Anyone interested in the British folk scene would be well advised to get hold of this free compilation which was available with ‘The Independent’ newspaper today, but has also been made free to download by the two labels concerned. Musicians include Lau, Mawkin:Causley, Spiers and Boden, Kris Drever, Rachel McShane and Chris Wood.

The link is http://bit.ly/2IBhc6.

Thanks for pointing us towards this, Floss. I know few of the artists, John McCusker, Sandy Wright and Kris Drever being exceptions.

McCusker is a fiddler who first came to prominence at a young age when he replaced Brian McNeill in Scotland’s Battlefield Band. I came across guitarist/singer Sandy Wright when we shared a concert billing of three duets: me (mandolin) with my guitarist, he with talented Scots fiddler Amy Geddes, and Brian Finnegan and Sarah Allen. A personal thrill for me was when we all joined up on stage at the end, me saying to myself, childlike, “I’m playing with Brian Finnegan!” over and over.

Kris Drever is a singer and guitar accompanist making waves in Scotland. He was part of the group Fine Friday with Anna-Wendy Stevenson (fiddle) and Nuala Kennedy (flute). Currently in acclaimed group Lau. His father, Ivan Drever from Orkney, is also a folk musician with many solo recordings and collaborations with duos and groups, most notably perhaps with fiddler Duncan Chisholm. One of Drever senior’s LPs can be seen in the music blog The Music Gatherer:
http://themusicgatherer.blogspot.com/2009/10/knowe-o-deil-vikings-bride-attic.html
