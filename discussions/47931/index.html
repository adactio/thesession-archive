Added by Rudall Cart on February 21st, 2023

Your favourite composer of Irish tunes?

This may sound ignorant but forgive me. It is only while trying to actually learn the names of some of my reels and jigs during lockdown that I started to appreciate the Irish tune composers who were not ‘lost to history’. I could see that there were tunes we played which were such close cousins of each other that they must have evolved out of earlier tunes which probably had no-one to credit, but I didn’t realise there were several good and distinctive writers who had completely entered the canon of session tunes.

I knew about O‘Carolan and I learned one tune as a child that someone told me was a ’Reavy’ tune, so I knew that at least was composed, but I honestly didnt know how many were written by people still alive now or alive when I learned them.

My current three favourite composers are Josie McDermott, Vincent Broderick and Maurice Lennon. Their tunes generally sit well on the flute and they have a lot of life to them. But who do you particularly love and why?

Re: Your favourite composer of Irish tunes?

Christy,

I think this is like one of those “Irish Tunes V Irish Music” discussions which we’ve had here before. Many tunes from all traditions along with new compositions become popular with Irish (style) musicians.
You don’t have to be Irish, of course, to be a good Irish musician and I once upset a few people here(many years ago) by apparently suggesting that this might be an advantage. :-) Of course, that wasn’t what I meant and my wording was rather clumsy. I did clarify things and apologise for the confusion later in the thread.

So, tunes which become popular in Irish sessions and by players of Irish Music could be composed anywhere and by anyone. In my opinion , it’s the style of music and how a tune is interpreted which is the important thing.

Conversely, music composed by an Irish person may or may not be Irish music as we would think of it. I suppose it could technically be called that because it was composed by somebody from Ireland but that’s not what we mean here.

For instance, to continue along the path of Trish’s(tongue in cheek) examples, Phil Coulter is an Irish musician. You could argue that “The Town I love so well” is an Irish song. However “Puppet on A String” is surely not. ;-)
