Lúnasa on the way out…

I generally try to get to a Lúnasa (or any trad band’s) gig at least twice when they do a tour here in the UK. I find the audience and venue can bring a variation in the concert. I have never been so disappointed in a group that I left during the interval. The first point being that we have been treated to a second rate version of the band; Tola Custy was standing in for Sean Smyth and Shane McGowan is in for Ed Boyd. The second is that the natural comedy element has gone between the band. Not that the comedy was what we went for but being a band without a vocalist they needed that something extra and now it seems to me and others that I spoke to that they have run their course. They did try to do something different with their last album but it really wasn’t anything that had not been done before and the big change in line up only makes you wonder about the many rumours in trad world that this is a sinking ship. I for one won’t be using my second lot of tickets (discount on eBay to follow) and from the foyer conversations I won’t be on my own. Will have to find my trad fix elsewhere ;)

In 17 years I have never seen a sub-standard Lúnasa show. That is the level of their commitment to their music, Irish music. For anyone interested in Irish traditional music to question a Lúnasa live show is ridiculous. They give it everything, every night. That is the essence of this band.

I manned the merchandise desk on both nights. It was such a buzz - people who had never seen the band before came up to me expressing their incredulity at this band they knew nothing about. I’m so proud to be associated with this band. I regard it as one of my life’s real privileges. I have no agenda for making this statement. I am moving on from management of Lúnasa after this tour so that I can focus on my music publishing activities which will involve promoting their dazzling music into the film and TV media.

My only reason to write this is that I have been made aware of this online dialogue and I also know where it came from. Tola Custy and Shane McGowan are with the Kev, Trevor and Cillian on this UK tour. Both are making their usual major contribution to the show. Lúnasa only work with the best. Nothing has changed or will change in this respect. These UK shows with this line up are some of the best I’ve seen. Many have made that same observation these past few nights.

Thanks for hearing me out and thanks from the lads for your support,

Stuart
