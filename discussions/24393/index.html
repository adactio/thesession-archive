Frustration!

So, I was moving a couple of cinder blocks out of the way to get to something in my storage shed, and, one false move later, I had slammed my ring finger between to the two of them and made myself incapable of playing any stringed instrument for who knows how long. I won’t go into disgusting details, but the end of my ring finger is swollen and quite painful. The doc said it may take quite some time to get back to normal.

What’s even more frustrating is that I had just had over a month of not being able to go to any sessions, had been learning a few tunes practicing along with some recordings that I was looking forward to playing in session the first chance I got, and blammo. It couldn’t be my picky, something I can get by somewhat without, no, it had to be my ring finger.

Well, I have a child being born in just under a month and lots to be dealing with no that, so maybe it’s the Supreme Being’s way of telling me I need to be concentrating on other things.

Sorry to take up a mustard board space for my belly-aching, but I felt like bemoaning my temporary fate a bit and seek a bit of commiseration from fellow musicians. In the scheme of things I suppose I shouldn’t feel too bad. I could be earning my living from music or have lost a finger altogether.
