Re: Celebrating St. Patrick ~ Composers in our midst

All this talk of copyright is a bit OTT. If someone was making money out of using another’s tunes it would be different. Or, and this does happen on here from time to time, taking someone’s compositions out of the (living) composer’s book, would be something that might lead to successful litigation. This thread is as good a place as any to bring this up, as it does concern contemporary compositions.
I don’t think any court is going to consider someone tweaking a tune that was submitted on a forum by the composer (especially one using an alias) as a serious breach of copyright. It’s where there is a pecuniary loss involved (and someone making money out of your tune is money that could have been in your pocket).
Imagine that someone brings out a book of their own compositions, with an aim to supplement their meagre income. Someone else comes along and posts all of those tunes on a website. Yes, there are people who would still buy the book, but there are many more who wouldn’t, as they can get them for nothing.
I remember a particular uilleann piper from the Rutherglen area who made it clear to me what he thought of several people copying out tunes from his book (and some of these were his arrangements of trad tunes). Re: Celebrating St. Patrick ~ Composers in our midst

From the start of this little annual celebration and reminder it was a nod of respect for those composers who chose to add ABCs for their inspirations to their details instead of making multiple submissions to the main tune database.

A few years back half a dozen of us had been regularly reminding composers, when they made a ‘my composition’ submission, to both own their submission, if they hadn’t, to take credit ~ and to check the FAQs. We’d more often than not quote Jeremy’s request/suggestion for a penance in the comments, to add at least 5 traditional transcriptions for every single submission of an own composition. Some folks didn’t like that, not the idea of penance, not the suggestion to consider the FAQs. Own compositions were quite often the first submission of any kind by a brand new member. More usually, or at least in my case, I’d also suggest they could enter ABCs for their compositions to their details, and any tale behind the inspiration would also be appreciated. Doing it that way there’s no expectation of penance, and no risk of attracting any ire from a few who did get a bit wound up over what was at times a plethora of first contribution “see what I’ve made”. Also, it wasn’t unusual for the ABCs to be bizarre. Someone should have made and archive for those entries, though some do survive on site. I tended to be on automatic, not fussed, not wound up, just quickly adding a reminder, the option, links. If there seemed to be a problem or a confusion with the ABCs I’d add relevant links to online guides. And, usually, I’d also send a transcribed attempt at clearing up any problem ABCs, saying that if it seemed right to them they were welcome to cut and pastee it if they wanted. Some have…

Most often any comment was after the 3rd compostion in a row, at least once after the 6th in a row, but also tended to happen when it was the first of any contribution made by a new arrival, with a “failte, but do read the FAQs…” Most were OK with it, but some few did take it quite hard… I don’t think anyone has bothered for at least a couple of years now. I haven’t…

I have always enjoyed exploring the inspiration of others, like them or not, and mostly not bothering to comment if I didn’t, while happily adding positive comment to say if I did.

Leaving the discussion side of things, but about ‘asking’, except where the tune is already in the database, when I’m adding it, I had been in discussion with the composer, and where it’s not a member, I’ve tried to contact them before submitting their tune, and have had some success there. Where it is a tune already in the database, when I’ve had some time with it and it has taken me here and there, I sometimes add a transcription, though rarely, and usually because I’ve enjoyed the tune, and added comment saying so. I’ve never been fond of the idea that things exist in a set form, and any one person picking up a tune, even trying to follow the dots note for note ~ it still comes out with unique features, though some rare souls believe that any variation or deviation is a sin. I’m not of that mind. I enjoy music too much to be tyrannical about it. They’re just melodies, wherever they rise from, and they have a life of their own once they’ve surfaced, from wherever. When one has given me some pleasure, sometimes, though not as often as some might think, I’ll notate where it has taken me and share that. I don’t really care if you like it or not. That’s your prerogative, as is to wail on about it if you allow yourself to get wound up about it. The world is full of all kinds, and directions, and notions of what’s right and wrong, and some folks obsess about it.

Every time I’ve started this thread I’ve made clear that it’s with the idea of giving comps a try and considering moving them to the database. Where I’ve done that, I’ve always included the original and said so, and credited the source, the composer. I’ve also only done so, in this particular instance, when I’ve liked their tune, enjoyed it. I’m not kickstarting anything, but read into things what you want, there the possibilities are infinite, and I really couldn’t give a damned when it gets this silly…
