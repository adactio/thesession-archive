No Direction Home

Just finished watching the first part of No Direction Home, the Martin Scorsese musical documentary about Bob Dylan….

-Wait a minute, I hear you say -what’s he got do with The Music?

-WhoTF is he? (from the younger generation.)

Well, he was mates with the Clancy Bros., and toured with them. Some of his songs were based on Irish and Scottish (and of course, American) folk songs. He was integral in the “Folk Revival” which started in the 60’s, from which, it could be argued, branched the traditional music revival.

“We owe so much to so few.” We cannot assume that someone else is gonna bring about a folk or trad revival just because the feeling’s there inside us pent up waiting to get out. It just might not happen. Eg, if Jeremy hadn’t invented *this* site, can we assume that someone else would have done so? Yeah, there are other trad music sites out there but none with the unique flavour of this board (IMHO).
(Honest, I’m not just currying favour with the Big J so he can let a non-The Music thread slip through!)

So. Did anyone else see it? (Part 2 is on Beeb2 tomorrow night…and the whole thing will be on some kinda general release soon.) Any views?
To keep it related to “our game”, why don’t we answer WRT the so-called Revival? But then, I guess it’s now over to you guys and guyesses….
