Christmas Quizz.

Please feel free - but not too free, because otherwise it’ll get anarchic, to upgrade and augment the rules of this wee Christmas game, as we go.

To start with, I suggest we each ask 5 questions - general the music questions about the music, not too geeky, but not too general. Let’s not get into posting abc’s then saying “name that tune!” - or ask too many questions that are easily sourced in the archives of the session - but I hope my questions don’t fall into that category…

Give it maybe 10-15 (to be decided) posts before you, the questioner, intervenes to give out your correct answers, and/or mete out your judgement.

This might be some fun…. (Gawd, I can see the hijacks mounting up and we haven’t even started yet!)

OK. My 5 questions, to get the ball rolling, so make them quite straightforward, are:

1. What is A/The Shaskeen/Seasguin?

2. Who wrote the jig “The Mist Covered Mountain”?

3. Who was Brigid of Knock, which the hornpipe comemmorates?

4. An easier one: Name the 2 major fiddle players from Sligo who recorded extensively in New York in the 1920’s and 30’s and who subsequently influenced back in Ireland until regional styles were rediscovered round about the late eighties of the previous century(to the present one).

5.Why is the Penny Whistle so-called?

Good Luck!

Danny
