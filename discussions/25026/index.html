ITM - Tune Intervals Challenge!

Way back (long ago) when I was at school, I had a music teacher who executed what we all considered at the time to be a sadistic form of torture.

He’d pick on someone at random, and summon them to the front of the class. Then he’d play a note on the piano, and command them to sing a named interval above it.

If you got it right, there was a grudging word of praise, and you were allowed to go back to your seat.

If you got it wrong, you were subjected to a lecture on the importance of recognising musical intervals - probably followed by a clip around the ear to drive the point home!

He did though supply us with list of well-known songs. In each case, the first two notes of the song was the interval required. If you memorised that list (and knew the songs) you could avoid that clip around the ear!

(In the case of the seventh interval, it was the first and third notes of the song. He didn’t know of a song where the interval between the first and second notes was a seventh.)

Here is the list:

Second - Frère Jacques

Third - While Shepherds’ Watched

Fourth - Auld Lang Syne

Fifth - Lavender’s Blue

Sixth - My Bonnie Lies Over the Ocean

Seventh - Bali Hai (from South Pacific musical)

Eighth - Somewhere Over the Rainbow

… But what’s all this got to do with ITM? Quite a lot, I think. At pub sessions, we are all “playing by ear”, and sometimes need to pick up and play a tune that we’ve never heard before. Recognising intervals is also a key to recognising the mode of a tune - an important consideration for guitar backers.

So, here’s my challenge, sessioneers - come up with a list of well-known ITM tunes that begin with those above intervals …
