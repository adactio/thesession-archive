All of these HTTP links reminds me of a joke.

An American walks into an Irish pub populated with several old Irishmen having a pint at the bar. It’s fairly silent until one of the old men says to his friends ‘39’, at which point the entire pub errupts in laughter. A few moments later another man exclaims ‘127’, and there is even more laughter.

The American is puzzled by this and asks one of the men if he could explain. ’Well“, says one of the men, ”It’s like this. We’ve known each other since we were kids and we’ve heard everyone’s jokes so many times before that we decided to give a number to each joke we know just to save a bit of time".

Facinated, the American asks if he could give it a try and the locals give him a nod. So the American says “13!”, and the pub is silent but for a few crickets in the corner. Flustered, the American loudly shouts “36!”, and receives only a polite chuckle. Desperate now, the American says “1864!” and the entire pub bursts into laughter!

After the laughter dies down, the American (now being fairly pleased with himself) asks “So, what joke did I say?”. One local replies, "I have no idea! We’ve never heard that one before!’
