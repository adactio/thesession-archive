What are your hobbies?

OK, its time for me to be a nosy ‘git’ again, and ask everyone another question about themselves. Reveal at your own discretion, or stay silent if you prefer. What are your hobbies, or things you do with your time besides your job and/or your music? (We won’t argue about whether or not music is a hobby or a job, I think we have already done that to death-but then again, maybe we will argue about it, you never know where these threads will go.) This is a diverse bunch of folks, and the jobs everyone revealed in the thread I started a while ago were very interesting. Since what is good for the goose is good for the gander--I will start off. When not working or playing music, I am usually playing golf (good Scottish game there), watching baseball (go Red Sox), or reading a book (mostly sea stories, science fiction or theology, these days).

To clarify for non-UK people, a caravan is a trailer in Americanese. A mobile home you put on the back of your car. Claro? But a static caravan is basically a loophole so you can have a cheap holiday home. So you buy one on a plot for 3,000-10,000 pounds, then you pay the rental of 1000 or 2,000PA and then you basically have two homes. If you get a nice quiet site such as ours, in rural coastal Suffolk, they are a fu<king great investment, ie weekend breaks are sorted…not to mention the quiet idyllic short holidays you can have. Especially if you have kid(s), particulary little ones. Ours is near Dunwich, actually very close to Dunwich Heath and Minsmere Bird Sanctuary.
So the “hobby” bit is that I am forever doing stuff to improve our caravanning “experience” -- stuff like:
Gas: propane or butane?
Lecky: only by 12 volt batteries but boosted by solar panel.
Fridge: gas powered.
Patio: I made one recently - a bit wonky but WTF
And so on. A constant labour of love.
200 yards from the beach. Sea vista from the cliffs equal the Cliffs of Moher..or is that Mohair. Sunsets to die for. Sunrises to wake up for! The night sky has more stars than black bits in between. The barbeques and cameraderie with our South London emigre gang, second to none (except going to sessions! - that’s an eternal!)
