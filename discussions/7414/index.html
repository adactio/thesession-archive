Sessions and that filthy habit of smoking

You can tell from the title that I’m a non smoker. I’m currently in the midst of the WORST althma attack of my entire life triggered by the smokers at the session I was at last Saturday. The only things keeping me going are (I suppose) the inhalers and other goodies they’ve put me on in the hospital.

So my questions are:

1. Are there more smokers or non smokers at your sessions?
2. Do the smokers light up right there in the music circle or do they go elsewhere?
3. Are the venues for your sessions still allowing smoking?
4. Are there any totally non smoking sessions around the world? I sure hope so cause my lungs have given me the BIG hint that they’re on strike in this regards.

I feel sorry for the smokers who truly want to quit but can’t seem to do it. BUT to anyone who smokes PLEASE know that your smoke is killing me and others like me. I for one would have a lung full of appreciation to those smokers who choose to go elsewhere to indulge their habit.

I love your music but I surely don’t want to die for it.

Hopefully I’ll be able to breathe again soon. It’s a habit I truly have grown accustomed to and hope to enjoy yet again.

Also for those of you who bring little kiddies to the sessions where there are smokers. My Mom and Dad used to smoke when I was a kid and that is why I’m having the problems I’m having now. Think of those kids and keep them well away from all that pollution. They’ll thank you for their clean little lungs.

Breathless Fluter
