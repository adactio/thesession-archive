Laddie w‘ th’ Plaidie

The tune “Laddie w‘ th’ Plaidie” was used by Edward Pursell in the 50s for his Gaelic Song - Am Faighir Muileach. It’s also known as Far Am Bi Na Faireachan which is the first line of the song itself.
