Mrs. Judge

I recall reading quite a while ago that O’Carolan wrote this in honor of his patron. When he was 18, he lost his sight due to smallpox. Mrs. Judge took him in and gave him room and board while he learned the harp.

↳ Posted by dano on May 25th, 2006.

Re: Mrs. Judge

Carolan’s patron was Mrs MacDermott Roe, his father was employed by her family in Co. Roscommon. When
he lost his sight at 18, she arranged for him to have harp lessons, then gave him money and a horse to embark
on his career as a travelling harper. It was to her house that he returned at the end of his life and he wrote
Carolan’s Farewell to Music there before he died. He wrote other tunes for her.
