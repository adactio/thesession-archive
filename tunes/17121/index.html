Sassafras Ass

Another quick reel by Irish whistle, mandolin and bouzouki player Andrei Coman. Sassafras is a type of tree similar to a poplar native to the eastern half of North America. Sassafras also, incidentally, rhymes with ass, leading to much hilarity on camping trips and hiking excursions.
