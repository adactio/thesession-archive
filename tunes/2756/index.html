New Ships A Sailing (reel)

This is a Fermanagh tune from the repertoire of legendary fiddle and flute duo, Mick Hoy and Eddie Duffy. It was recorded by Frankie Kennedy on one of Altan’s early recordings, and most recently by Laurence Nugent on Hidden Fermanagh Vol.1.

↳ Posted by slainte on May 11th, 2007.
