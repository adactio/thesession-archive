Composed by Tony “Sully” Sullivan. I think the title refers to flute-player Mick Moriarty who used to play a lot with Sully.

↳ Posted by Kenny on October 11th, 2007.

I think the extra bar is at the end of the 1st part, not the end of the tune, otherwise it doesn’t make as much sense structurally as far as part endings and cadences are concerned.
