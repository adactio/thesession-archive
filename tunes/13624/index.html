One comment

Irene Anderson’s

I required a tune to precede “Calum’s Road” (https://thesession.org/tunes/3016) and I wasn’t really satisfied with anything, so I wrote this myself for the purpose. The truth is, I quite like it. Irene Anderson, originally from Aberdeenshire, has lived in Crieff (Perthshire) for many years, and plays piano accordion. I have had the pleasure of playing with her in dance bands for the last few years.

Like “Calum’s Road” this isn’t really a Strathspey but a march.
