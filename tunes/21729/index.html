Re: Nancy Clough

Seems a very strange version to me.
Nancy Clough is played very frequently up here in Northumbria where it is usually just called Nancy.
Of course its not a reel but a rant - sort of half way between a reel and a polka but with the beat at a fairly comfortable pace of 100-120 bpm.
Nancy was the wife of Tom Clough the Northumbrian piper who composed the tune.
