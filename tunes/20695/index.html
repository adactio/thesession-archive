↳ Posted by Kevin Rowsome on April 24th, 2021.

Re: The First Friday

Some new tunes - the very best new tunes - come box fresh with a patina of age. They should have been around forever, but haven’t been and when they emerge they meet a need which we didn’t know, or maybe a need which we “felt” but couldn’t articulate. Such is this tune. Just lovely - and one which, in my humble opinion, deserves to be picked up (and not just by pipers) to become a standard…
