The Butchers Of Bristol

A tune that was played at a session in Bristol UK that I attended yesterday evening.
NOT the same as a tune of the this name already posted to session.org.

The Butchers Of Bristol, X:2

Apparently, it’s in the William Irwin MS, c1850, (untitled) in the key of G Dorian. Also in other old manuscripts entitled “The Butchers of Bristol” or “Barbara Allen”

Played by Stepling at the Bath Folk Festival in 2016
