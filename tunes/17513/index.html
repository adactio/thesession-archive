Two comments

Ard Rí

I got this tune from the album Zero of the Irish Concertina Ensemble. “Ard Rí” was composed by Tim Collins to commemorate the millennial anniversary of the death of Brian Boru. It is a bit rangy but a gorgeous concertina tune, I find. I’m not 100% sure about the tune type, it could be either a march or a slow reel but I’m open to discussions :)
